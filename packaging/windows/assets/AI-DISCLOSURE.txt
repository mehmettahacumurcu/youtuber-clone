AI-GENERATED PERSONA AND VOICE DISCLOSURE

YouTuber Studio is a research and fan-development system that generates text
and audio with machine-learning models adapted from a real public creator's
published material. The creator has granted permission for this project.

Outputs are synthetic. They are not new genuine statements, opinions,
endorsements, or recordings by the target speaker. Knowledge-grounded answers
may summarize retrieved transcript evidence; an "answer anyway" mode can also
generate material without supporting evidence. Users must keep this disclosure
visible in public demonstrations, must not use the software for deception,
fraud, harassment, political persuasion, or impersonation, and must follow all
applicable voice, publicity, copyright, privacy, and deepfake laws.
